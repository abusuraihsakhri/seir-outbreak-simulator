Interpretation and limitations

Deterministic, homogeneous-mixing model with a fixed population, permanent recovery, no births or deaths, and a single permanent contact-rate change. Vaccination confers sterilizing protection at day zero; protection is modeled as coverage × efficacy.

“Cumulative infected” includes exposed, currently infectious, and recovered individuals at the final simulated day; it is not a forecast of reported cases. SIR ignores the initial exposed input. Vaccination scenarios use SEIRV, so comparison with a SIR baseline also changes model structure.

For research and teaching only. Not calibrated to surveillance data and not for clinical or public-health decisions. All computations and CSV export run in your browser; no input is transmitted to a server.
